# Cement Bricks Accounts System

A simple and user-friendly web-based accounting and business management system designed for cement brick manufacturers and small business owners.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Cement Bricks Accounts System** helps brick manufacturing businesses digitally manage their daily business activities such as sales, production, transport, worker wages, expenses, and profit analysis.

The system reduces manual paperwork, improves calculation accuracy, and provides a centralized dashboard for monitoring business performance.

## 📊 Main Modules

### 1. Dashboard

Displays important business information such as:

* Total Sales
* Paid Sales
* Unpaid Sales
* Total Profit
* Current Profit
* Production Wages
* Total Expenses
* Transport Profit

### 2. Sales Management

Records customer sales information including:

* Date
* Customer
* Place
* Driver
* Vehicle
* Brick Type
* Quantity
* Rate
* Transport Charges
* Paid / Due Status

### 3. Production

Helps maintain production-related records and track manufactured brick quantities.

### 4. Worker Wages

Manages production worker payments based on work completed and applicable rates.

### 5. Transport

Tracks lorry transportation activities, including self-use and outside transportation.

### 6. Expenses

Records business expenses such as:

* Diesel
* Cement
* Forklift expenses
* Lorry expenses
* Tea and other small expenses

### 7. Reports and Analysis

Provides business analysis through charts, summaries, and reports to help understand sales, expenses, wages, and profitability.

## 💾 Data Management

The application supports digital record management and can use browser local storage for maintaining data.

Excel functionality is provided through **SheetJS** for importing and exporting business records.

## ▶️ How to Run

### Method 1: Using VS Code

1. Open the project folder in **Visual Studio Code**.
2. Open `index.html`.
3. Use the **Live Server** extension.
4. Right-click `index.html`.
5. Select **Open with Live Server**.

### Method 2: Browser

You can also open:

```text
index.html
```

directly in a web browser.

## 🔥 Firebase Configuration

If Firebase features are enabled, configure the Firebase project in:

```text
firebase-config.js
```

Add your Firebase project configuration according to your Firebase project settings.
